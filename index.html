clipbot

A personal, self-hosted tool that turns popular livestream moments into short vertical clips and publishes them to its owner's own channels.

Privacy: clipbot only ever signs in to the owner's own accounts. Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.

What clipbot does

  1. Finds popular viewer-made clips of livestreams (via the official Twitch API) and highlights viewers point to.
  2. Edits each moment into a 9:16 short: facecam/gameplay layout, captions, a short hook, trimmed silences.
  3. Runs a local safety and quality check; anything borderline is held for manual review.
  4. Writes a title and description that always credit the streamer with a link to their channel.
  5. Gives the owner a review window (keep / reject) before anything is published.
  6. Publishes the approved clips on a fixed schedule to the owner's own channels.

How clipbot uses YouTube

Uploading to YouTube is one feature of clipbot. It uses YouTube API Services to upload the finished clips to the owner's own two YouTube channels (about six Shorts per channel per day), scheduled with a publish time, and to read those videos' public statistics (views, likes, watch time).

clipbot is an independent personal project. It is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by YouTube or Google. By using clipbot's YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Who uses clipbot

clipbot is operated by one person for their own channels. It is not offered as a service to other users and does not access anyone else's account.
