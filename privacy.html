Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what information clipbot accesses, stores and shares. clipbot is a personal tool run by a single operator (the "owner") on the owner's own computer. It only signs in to accounts that belong to the owner. You must agree to this policy before using clipbot's features.

YouTube API Services

clipbot uses YouTube API Services. By using clipbot's YouTube features you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What information clipbot accesses

  • OAuth access tokens for the owner's own YouTube channels, granted through Google's sign-in. clipbot never asks for or stores a YouTube or Google password.
  • Upload access (scope youtube.upload): to upload the owner's finished clips with title, description, tags and a scheduled publish time.
  • Read access to the owner's own videos and channel statistics (scopes youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly): views, likes, comments and average watch time of the videos clipbot uploaded, used to see which kinds of clips perform best.

clipbot does not access any other person's YouTube account, private data, subscribers' personal information, comments' authors, or messages.

What clipbot stores, and where

  • The OAuth tokens are stored in a local file on the owner's computer (and as an encrypted repository secret in the owner's private GitHub repository, as a backup).
  • The IDs of uploaded videos, their scheduled times and their statistics are stored in local files on the owner's computer.
  • YouTube statistics (views, likes, comments, average watch time) are only collected for videos published in the last 30 days and are refreshed every few hours. Statistics that are older than 30 days, or that could not be refreshed within 30 days, are deleted automatically.
  • The summary of which kinds of clips perform best is calculated from the last 30 days of YouTube statistics only. It is recalculated regularly, and a summary older than 30 days is deleted instead of used.
  • Rendered clip files are deleted from the owner's computer two days after publishing.

How information is used and shared

The information is used only to upload the owner's clips and to measure their performance for the owner. clipbot does not sell, rent or share this information with anyone. No third party receives it, and clipbot shows no advertising. The project website (this site) uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking.

How to revoke access and delete data

  • Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • When access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and clipbot deletes the stored tokens and all stored YouTube data (video IDs and statistics) within 7 days.
  • Deletion of all stored data can also be requested at any time by email to gta6knowitall@gmail.com; it is completed within 7 days.

Contact

Questions, complaints or deletion requests about privacy: email gta6knowitall@gmail.com.
