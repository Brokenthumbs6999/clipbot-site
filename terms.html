Effective date: 23 November 2026

1. What clipbot is

clipbot is a personal, self-hosted tool operated by a single person (the "owner") to publish short, edited livestream highlight clips to the owner's own social media channels. It is not offered as a service to the public.

2. YouTube

clipbot uses YouTube API Services. Anyone using clipbot's YouTube features agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's processing of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. clipbot is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by YouTube or Google.

3. TikTok

clipbot posts to the owner's own TikTok accounts with TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API. Use of these features is subject to the TikTok Terms of Service. Every TikTok post is made by the owner on clipbot's posting screen, where the owner chooses who can view the video, whether comments, duets and stitches are allowed, and whether the video promotes a brand. By posting, the owner agrees to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation and, for branded content, TikTok's Branded Content Policy. clipbot adds no watermarks or promotional branding to posted videos. clipbot is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by TikTok.

4. Instagram

clipbot publishes Reels to the owner's own Instagram professional accounts with the Instagram Graph API. Use of these features is subject to the Instagram Terms of Use and Meta Platform Terms. clipbot is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Instagram or Meta.

5. Content and credit

  • Every published clip credits the original streamer by name with a link to their channel.
  • Clips are edited (cropped to 9:16, captioned, trimmed) short excerpts of popular public livestream moments.
  • Clips are checked for nudity, sexual content, graphic violence and hateful content before publishing, and the owner can reject any clip before it goes public.
  • If a streamer or rights holder objects to a clip, it is removed. Requests: see Contact below.

6. Privacy

How information is accessed, stored and deleted is described in the Privacy Policy.

clipbot is provided as is, without warranty of any kind.
